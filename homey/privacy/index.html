Homey Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Homey is made by MagnIT AB, a Swedish company. Everything you add to Homey stays on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, so your records and documents never reach us. We don’t sell data, show ads or use analytics or tracking. On Android, Google’s document scanner sends Google technical usage data, as explained below.

What Homey stores on your device

  • Homes: each home’s name and currency, and any details you add.
  • Projects: titles, notes, dates, who did the work and what it cost.
  • Documents: the PDFs, photos and scans you add, with their titles and descriptions. These can contain anything you choose to keep in them, such as names, addresses and prices on a receipt or deed.

All of this is stored in Homey’s private storage on your device. We can’t see it, and Homey doesn’t send it anywhere. It works without an internet connection.

Camera, photos and files

Homey asks for camera access to scan and photograph documents. Without it, Homey still works, but you can’t add documents with the camera. You can withdraw it at any time in your device’s settings. Homey doesn’t use the microphone.

Homey only receives the photos and files you pick or share to it, not the rest of your photo library or files. Web links and text shared to Homey are ignored. When you share a document from Homey, it goes to the app or person you choose.

Document scanner

On iPhone and iPad, scanning uses Apple’s built-in document camera. On Android, it uses Google’s ML Kit document scanner, delivered through Google Play services. Both work on your device: your scans and documents aren’t sent to Apple or Google.

On Android, ML Kit sends Google technical information so that Google can measure, maintain and improve it and detect misuse. According to Google, this kind of information includes your device’s manufacturer, model and operating system version, Homey’s package name and version, device or installation identifiers, performance measurements, image format and resolution, and error codes. ML Kit may also contact Google for bug fixes and updates. This is the only data that leaves your device through Homey without you choosing to send it. It doesn’t go through us, and we can’t see it. See Google’s privacy policy.

Device backups

Homey’s data, including your documents, can be part of your device’s own backup: iCloud on iPhone and iPad, and Google’s backup on Android. If you’ve turned these backups on, copies are stored in your Apple or Google account under Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy. We have no access to them. You can turn these backups off in your device’s settings. Android backs up at most 25 MB per app; above that, none of Homey’s data is backed up.

App stores

Apple and Google handle your download of Homey under their own privacy policies. They give us statistics about installs, and may give us crash reports from people who’ve agreed to share them. These don’t include your records or documents.

Who we share data with

Nobody. We don’t receive your records or documents, so we have nothing to share or sell. Apple and Google handle data as described above, in their own services.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. In the app, you can delete a document, a project with or without its documents, or a home with everything in it. Deleting the app removes all of Homey’s data from your device. We can’t delete it for you or get it back, because we don’t have it.

Copies in your iCloud or Google backup are kept and deleted according to those services, and you can delete them there. To ask Google about the data it receives from ML Kit, see Google’s privacy policy.

Your rights

You control your data in Homey directly, in the app. Because we hold no records about you, there’s nothing in Homey for us to copy, correct or delete, but you’re welcome to email us with questions. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) at imy.se.

Security

Homey’s data is kept in app storage that other apps can’t read, and your device’s lock protects it. Homey doesn’t add a lock or encryption of its own, so anyone who can unlock your device can open Homey.

Children

Homey is made for adults keeping records of their home. It isn’t aimed at children.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
