Home records · iPhone, iPad and Android

The lasting record of your home.

Keep what was done to your home, when, by whom and at what cost, with the receipts and papers that prove it.

App Store Google Play

Free. No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device and works offline.

How it works

  1. Add your home

    Give it a name and its currency. Add the address, the year it was built or anything else worth keeping, as details in your own words.

  2. Record what’s been done

    A new roof, a heat pump, a kitchen: note what was done, who did it and what it cost, dated as precisely as you remember.

  3. Keep the papers with it

    Scan the receipt, photograph the warranty or import the PDF, and file each document on the home or on its project.

Everything about the house, in one place

  • A timeline of projects

    Each home opens on its projects, year by year, with the newest first.

  • Dated as you remember

    A month, a range of months, or only when it was finished. A project with no date at all is fine too.

  • Scan, photograph or import

    Use the document scanner, the camera, Files or your photo library, or share PDFs and pictures to Homey from another app.

  • Any home, anywhere

    No country-specific forms. Each home keeps the details you choose, in its own currency.

  • Search it all

    Find a project or a document by its title, notes or description, or a home by its details.

  • Pass it on

    Send a receipt to your insurer or a floor plan to a builder straight from Homey, and keep a holiday home alongside your own.

For the day you need it

  • Selling the house

    Show buyers what’s been done and when, with the papers to back it up.

  • Claiming on insurance

    Find the receipt and the date of the work without digging through drawers.

  • Who fixed the roof?

    Call the same firm again, or look up the warranty that came with the job.

Your home stays yours

Homey has no account, no server and no tracking: the app sends us nothing. Your records and documents stay on your device, and can be part of its own iCloud or Google backup. On Android, Google’s document scanner sends Google some technical usage data. The privacy policy has the details.
