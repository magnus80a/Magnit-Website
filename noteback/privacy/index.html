NoteBack Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

NoteBack is made by MagnIT AB. We don’t collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs.

Microphone

NoteBack uses the microphone to analyze sound in real time. The audio is processed on your device only. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. You can turn off microphone access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.

Settings

Your settings are stored locally on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Tips

Tips are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. We don’t receive your name, payment details or any other personal information. See Apple’s privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Data retention and deletion

Since we don’t collect any data, there is nothing for us to keep or delete.
