How it works

  1. Post a game

    Pick the game, the time, the place and how many seats there are. Make it public, or invite-only and share the link.

  2. Players join

    Players nearby find it in Discover and take a seat. If you’d rather choose, approve each one first.

  3. Meet up and play

    Chat with your players before the day, post updates and add the game to your calendar.

Made for the one who gathers the table

  • Games near you

    Discover shows games around you with distances, or in any city you pick. Location access is optional.

  • A waiting list that moves

    When a seat opens, the next player on the waiting list gets it automatically.

  • Vote on a date

    Not sure when? Propose a few dates for an invite-only game and let your players vote.

  • A chat for every game

    The organiser and the players get their own chat, with push notifications if you want them.

  • You decide who plays

    Approve players before they join, show the address to players only, and block or report anyone who spoils the fun.

  • Share it as a link

    Send an invitation or a public game as a link, and it opens straight in the app.
