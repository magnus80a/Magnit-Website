Delete your JoinUp account

Last updated: October 15, 2026

JoinUp is made by MagnIT AB. You can delete your JoinUp account and its data at any time, in the app or by email. Deletion is permanent.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open JoinUp and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete permanently.

Your account is deleted right away and you’re signed out.

Delete it without the app

  1. Email support.joinup.magnit@gmail.com from the address you sign in to JoinUp with.
  2. Use the subject “Delete my JoinUp account”.

We delete your account within 30 days and reply when it’s done. Then we delete our email conversation too. You don’t need to install the app.

What we delete

  • Your account: email address, password and sign-in sessions.
  • Your profile: display name and city.
  • The games you organise, with their details, addresses, date polls, invitation links, updates, chats and player lists. They’re removed for every player. Confirmed and waiting players of upcoming games are told that the game was cancelled because you deleted your account.
  • Your seats, date votes and waiting-list places in other people’s games.
  • Your chat messages, with earlier versions of the ones you edited, how far you’ve read each chat, and any mutes or exclusions organisers set for you.
  • Your notifications, your notification settings and your devices’ push notification tokens.
  • Your acceptance of the terms, the players you’ve blocked, and other players’ blocks of you.

What we keep, and for how long

Each confirmed or waiting player of an upcoming game keeps a cancellation notice with the game’s title and its date, if one was set. It keeps no organiser profile details, place or address. Only the player receiving the notice can read it, and it is deleted when that player deletes their account.

A few records stay with the services JoinUp runs on, and are deleted automatically:

  • Security logs of sign-ins and other account activity, with your account ID, email address, IP address and device information. Supabase, our database host, deletes them within 7 days.
  • Database backups, which may contain your data. Supabase replaces them within 7 days.
  • Copies of the emails we sent you, such as sign-up confirmations and password resets. Resend, our email service, deletes them after 30 days.

We also keep reports you made, or that were made about you or the games you organised, with a copy of what was reported and what we did about it. We keep them as long as we need them to deal with repeated abuse.

We don’t keep anything else. Push notifications about your chat messages that already reached other players’ phones stay there until those players clear them.
