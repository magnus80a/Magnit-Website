JoinUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

JoinUp is made by MagnIT AB, a Swedish company that is responsible for your personal data in JoinUp. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it and how to delete it. We don’t sell your data, show ads or use analytics or tracking.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password.
  • Profile: your display name and, if you add it, your city.
  • Games you organise: title, game, description, tags, date and time or proposed dates, place name, city, address and map location, player limits, updates you post to players, and invitation links.
  • Games you join: your seats, waiting-list places and date votes.
  • Game chats: the messages you write in your games’ chats, earlier versions of messages you edit, and how far you’ve read each chat. If you organise a game, the players you mute in its chat or exclude from the game.
  • Notifications: notifications about your games, whether you want push notifications for chat messages, and, if you allow push notifications, your devices’ push notification tokens.
  • Terms: which version of the terms and community guidelines you accepted, and when.
  • Blocked players: the players you block.
  • Reports: when you report a game, a player, an organiser’s update or a chat message, your reason, your note if you add one, and a copy of what you reported.
  • Moderation: if we remove something you posted or suspend your account, what we did and why.
  • Sign-ins: the IP address and device information of each signed-in session, and security logs of sign-ins and other account activity.

Your location

If you allow location access, JoinUp uses your location on your phone to show nearby games and how far away they are. To show your city’s name, your phone’s built-in map service (Apple’s on iPhone, Google’s on Android) looks up your location. JoinUp’s servers never receive or store your location.

Without location access, Discover starts with the city in your profile, without distances. If you haven’t added a city, it starts with All places. You can choose a city with games or All places without sharing your location. Choosing where to browse doesn’t change your profile city.

When you search for a city in Discover, your phone’s map service looks up the city name you enter. If you choose a search result, distances are measured from that city’s centre. On Android, searching for a city needs location access.

Who can see your information

  • Other players see your display name and city when they can see a game you organise or join.
  • Public games are visible to everyone who uses JoinUp. Invite-only games are visible only to their players and to people with the invitation link.
  • If you show a game’s address only to players, everyone else sees just the place name, city and approximate distance.
  • Organisers see who has joined their games and who’s on the waiting list.
  • A game’s chat is visible only to its organiser and its confirmed and waiting players, also when the game is public. Players who leave the game can’t read it any more, and you don’t see messages from players you block.
  • Only you see the players you block. They aren’t told.
  • Only we see reports. Nobody is told who reported them, though an organiser is told when a message in their game’s chat is reported. To deal with a report about a message, we read the game’s chat, including earlier versions of edited messages.

Services we use

These companies process data on our behalf, only to run JoinUp:

  • Supabase stores JoinUp’s data and handles accounts and sign-ins.
  • Resend sends sign-up confirmation and password reset emails, and emails us new reports.
  • Expo sends push notifications through Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. A notification shows a game’s title and what happened, such as a confirmed seat, a new date or an organiser’s update. A push notification about a chat message also shows its author’s display name and the message’s text, shortened if it’s long, so that text passes through Expo and Apple or Google on its way to other players’ phones. You can turn these off under Profile > Notifications.

All three are based in the United States. When your data leaves the EU/EEA, it’s protected by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Apple and Google handle some data under their own privacy policies. Your phone’s map service looks up the addresses organisers enter and the city names you search for in Discover, and View location opens a game’s place in Apple Maps or Google Maps. Emails you send us go to a Gmail account, so Google handles them too.

Why we use your data

  • To run JoinUp for you: your account, games, chats, notifications and emails. The legal basis is our agreement with you when you create an account.
  • To keep JoinUp secure and stop misuse: sign-in sessions and security logs. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure service.
  • To keep JoinUp safe for players: your acceptance of the terms, the players you block, reports, and our removals and suspensions. We also check the text you post against a list of words that aren’t allowed. The legal basis is our agreement with you, our legitimate interest in a safe community and our duties under the EU Digital Services Act.
  • Location and push notifications: to help you find a local game and only with your permission, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone’s settings.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile, games, notifications, notification settings, accepted terms and blocked players: until you delete your account. Games you’ve joined are also removed if their organiser deletes their account.
  • Game chats: nobody can write in a chat three days after the game, and its messages, earlier versions of edited messages, how far players have read and mutes are deleted 30 days after that. A deleted message loses its text right away, and so do its earlier versions. Your messages are also deleted when you delete your account.
  • If an organiser deletes their account, confirmed and waiting players of their upcoming games keep a notice saying the game was cancelled because the organiser deleted their account. It keeps the game’s title and its date, if one was set, but no organiser profile details, place or address. Only the player receiving the notice can read it, and it is deleted when that player deletes their account.
  • Reports and what we did about them: as long as we need them to deal with repeated abuse, also after the accounts involved are deleted.
  • Sign-in sessions, with IP address and device information: until you sign out or delete your account.
  • Push notification tokens: until you sign out on that device. Records of sent push notifications: 7 days, but what a push notification said is deleted once it’s sent. Push notifications that reached a phone stay there until its owner clears them.
  • Security logs and database backups: up to 7 days.
  • Copies of emails we sent you, at Resend: 30 days.
  • Emails you send us: as long as we need them to help you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, and ask us to correct it, delete it, limit how we use it or give it to you in a portable format. You can also object to how we use it. Email us at the address below. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) at imy.se.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time under Profile > Delete account in the app, or by email. See how to delete your account, and what we delete and keep.

Security

Everything between the app and our servers is encrypted. Passwords are stored as secure hashes, never in plain text, and database rules make sure players only see what they’re allowed to see.

Children

JoinUp isn’t meant for children under 13.

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
