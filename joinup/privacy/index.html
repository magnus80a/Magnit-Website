JoinUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

JoinUp is made by MagnIT AB, a Swedish company that is responsible for your personal data in JoinUp. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it and how to delete it. We don’t sell your data, show ads or use analytics or tracking.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password.
  • Profile: your display name and, if you add it, your city.
  • Games you organise: title, game, description, tags, date and time or proposed dates, place name, city, address and map location, player limits, updates you post to players, and invitation links.
  • Games you join: your seats, waiting-list places and date votes.
  • Notifications: notifications about your games and, if you allow push notifications, your devices’ push notification tokens.
  • Sign-ins: the IP address and device information of each signed-in session, and security logs of sign-ins and other account activity.

Your location

If you allow location access, JoinUp uses your location on your phone to show how far away games are. To show your city’s name, your phone’s built-in map service (Apple’s on iPhone, Google’s on Android) looks up your location. JoinUp’s servers never receive or store your location. Without location access, JoinUp shows games without distances.

Who can see your information

  • Other players see your display name and city when they can see a game you organise or join.
  • Public games are visible to everyone who uses JoinUp. Invite-only games are visible only to their players and to people with the invitation link.
  • If you show a game’s address only to players, everyone else sees just the place name, city and approximate distance.
  • Organisers see who has joined their games and who’s on the waiting list.

Services we use

These companies process data on our behalf, only to run JoinUp:

  • Supabase stores JoinUp’s data and handles accounts and sign-ins.
  • Resend sends sign-up confirmation and password reset emails.
  • Expo sends push notifications through Apple Push Notification service and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. A notification shows a game’s title and what happened, such as a confirmed seat, a new date or an organiser’s update.

All three are based in the United States. When your data leaves the EU/EEA, it’s protected by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Apple and Google handle some data under their own privacy policies. Your phone’s map service finds the addresses organisers enter, and View location opens a game’s place in Apple Maps or Google Maps.

Why we use your data

  • To run JoinUp for you: your account, games, notifications and emails. The legal basis is our agreement with you when you create an account.
  • To keep JoinUp secure and stop misuse: sign-in sessions and security logs. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure service.
  • Location and push notifications: to help you find a local game and only with your permission, which you can withdraw at any time in your phone’s settings.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile, games and notifications: until you delete your account. Games you’ve joined are also removed if their organiser deletes their account.
  • Sign-in sessions, with IP address and device information: until you sign out or delete your account.
  • Push notification tokens: until you sign out on that device. Records of sent push notifications: 7 days.
  • Security logs and database backups: up to 7 days.
  • Copies of emails we sent you, at Resend: 30 days.
  • Emails you send us: as long as we need them to help you.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time under Profile > Delete account in the app, or by email. See how to delete your account, and what we delete and keep.

Security

Everything between the app and our servers is encrypted. Passwords are stored as secure hashes, never in plain text, and database rules make sure players only see what they’re allowed to see.

Children

JoinUp isn’t meant for children under 13.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
