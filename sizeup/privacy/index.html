SizeUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

SizeUp is made by MagnIT AB, a Swedish company. Everything you add to SizeUp stays on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and the app doesn’t connect to the internet, so your projects, photos and measurements never reach us. The app has no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs, and we don’t sell data.

What SizeUp stores on your device

  • Projects: the name of each project and the order of its photos.
  • Photos: a copy of each photo you take or pick, and a small preview of it.
  • Measurements and notes: the arrows, values, units, colours and text notes you draw on each photo, and the title you give it.
  • Settings: your default colour and PDF paper size.

All of this is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. We can’t see it, and SizeUp doesn’t send it anywhere.

Camera and photos

SizeUp asks for camera access the first time you tap Take a photo. It uses the camera only to take the photo you draw on. Without it, you can still use photos from your library. You can withdraw access at any time in your device’s settings. SizeUp doesn’t use the microphone or your location.

When you pick a photo from your library, your device’s own photo picker hands over only the photo you choose, not the rest of your library. SizeUp saves its own copy and leaves the original in your library as it is.

On iPhone, SizeUp asks to add to your photo library only if you choose Save Image while sharing a photo. It can add that photo but can’t read your library.

Sharing

When you share a photo as a JPEG or a project as a PDF, SizeUp creates the file on your device and hands it to your device’s share sheet. The app or person you choose there receives it under their own terms. SizeUp doesn’t send anything by itself.

Contact us in Settings opens a new email in your mail app. We receive only what you choose to send.

Device backups

SizeUp’s data, including your photos, can be part of your device’s own backup: iCloud on iPhone, and Google’s backup on Android. If you’ve turned these backups on, copies are stored in your Apple or Google account under Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy. We have no access to them. You can turn these backups off in your device’s settings.

App stores

SizeUp is bought through the App Store or Google Play, which Apple and Google handle under their own privacy policies. We don’t receive your name or payment details. Apple and Google give us statistics about downloads, and may give us crash reports from people who’ve agreed to share them. These don’t include your projects, photos or measurements.

Who we share data with

Nobody. We don’t receive your data, so we have nothing to share or sell. Apple and Google handle data as described above, in their own services.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. In the app, you can delete a single photo with everything drawn on it, or a whole project with all its photos. Deleting the app removes all of SizeUp’s data from your device. We can’t delete it for you or get it back, because we don’t have it.

Copies in your iCloud or Google backup, and files you’ve shared or saved to your photo library, are kept until you delete them there.

Your rights

You control your data in SizeUp directly, in the app. Because we hold no records about you, there’s nothing for us to copy, correct or delete, but you’re welcome to email us with questions. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) at imy.se.

Security

SizeUp’s data is kept in app storage that other apps can’t read, and your device’s lock protects it. The app doesn’t add a lock or encryption of its own, so anyone who can unlock your device can open it.

Children

SizeUp is a general tool app. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
