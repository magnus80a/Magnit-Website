Learn it by ear, one bar at a time

  1. Bring in the song

    From Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox, your Music library, AirDrop or Finder on your computer. One song or a whole folder at once.

  2. Slow it down and loop it

    Drop the tempo, shift the key to your tuning or your voice, and loop the part your hands don’t know yet.

  3. Come back tomorrow

    Tempo, key, marks, the loop and your place are where you left them, so you pick up right where you stopped.

Everything on one screen

  • Slow down, same key

    Set the tempo anywhere from 25% to 200%. Shift the key up to an octave either way, in semitones or fine steps. Both change instantly while the song plays.

  • Loops with no gap

    One tap loops the section you’re in, or tap Start and End as a phrase goes by. Drag the edges to fit. It repeats back to back, with no gap and no click.

  • Marks you can name

    Drop a mark at the verse, the bridge or the bar you keep missing without stopping the music, and jump between them from the main screen.

  • Skip what you don’t play

    Leave out the long intro, someone else’s solo or the fade-out. Playback jumps straight past them.

  • See the whole song

    A zoomable waveform scrolls under a fixed playhead. Scrub it with a finger, or jump anywhere from the overview strip.

  • Time to pick up your instrument

    Play counts down 2, 1 on screen before the song starts, so your hands are ready when it comes in.

  • Playlists for rehearsal

    Put songs in order for a rehearsal or a set and play straight through, each at its own tempo and key.

  • Made for iPad too

    On iPad your marks sit in a column beside the waveform, with keyboard shortcuts and trackpad support.

  • Keep going with the screen locked

    Play and pause from the lock screen, Control Center or your headphones, without unlocking your phone.

Works with MP3, AAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF and FLAC. Songs from an Apple Music subscription are copy-protected, so no app can import them. Songs you bought from the iTunes Store or have as your own files work.

Repa has no account, no ads, no analytics and never goes online. Your songs, marks and playlists stay on your iPhone or iPad. The privacy policy says it in full.
