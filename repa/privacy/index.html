Repa Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Repa is a music practice player for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by MagnIT AB. We don’t collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, analytics or ads, and Repa itself sends nothing off your device. The only network use is the App Store or Google Play handling your purchase.

Music library

On iPhone and iPad, if you import songs from your device’s music library, Repa asks for access to it. The app only reads the songs you pick, and nothing about your library is sent anywhere. You can turn off access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Media & Apple Music.

On Android, Repa doesn’t ask for access to your music or your files. Android’s own file picker hands Repa only the songs or the folder you choose.

Songs and files

Songs you import are copied into the app on your device: on iPhone and iPad from Files, iCloud Drive, other storage apps or AirDrop, and on Android from your phone’s storage, an SD card, a storage app, or another app through Open with or Share. Repa only opens the files you choose. Your songs are never uploaded or shared.

Marks, playlists and settings

Your marks, loops, playlists and per-song settings are stored locally on your device. Everything is removed when you delete the app.

On iPhone and iPad, imported songs and this data are included in your device’s iCloud or computer backups, handled by Apple, so they come back if you restore your device. On Android, if you have Google’s backup turned on, it holds your songs’ details, marks, playlists and settings, but not the songs’ audio; after a restore, Repa asks you to add the files again. These backups are kept in your Apple or Google account, under Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy, and we have no access to them.

Purchase

Repa is free to download and keeps 3 songs. One purchase, Repa Unlimited, removes the limit. It is bought through the App Store or Google Play, which handle the payment entirely, and Repa itself sends nothing. Repa asks the store whether you own Repa Unlimited and keeps that answer on your device; on Android it isn’t part of the backup. We don’t receive your name, payment details or any other personal information. See Apple’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

On Android, Repa sells Repa Unlimited through Google’s Play Billing Library, which is part of the app. The library sends Google its own diagnostic reports on how the purchase service is working, such as whether its requests to Google Play succeed or fail and any connection problems, over an encrypted connection. It can’t be turned off in the app, contains nothing about your songs, and we don’t receive it. Google handles it under Google’s privacy policy.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. On Android, Google may do the same if you share usage and diagnostics with Google. They contain no personal information. You can change this in your device’s settings: on iPhone and iPad in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Repa isn’t designed for children under 13, and it collects no information from anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Since we don’t collect any data, there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Delete a song in Repa, or delete Repa, and it is gone from your device. The Play Billing Library’s reports are Google’s: Google’s privacy policy says how to ask Google about them.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
