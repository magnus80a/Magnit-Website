Repa Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Repa is made by MagnIT AB. We don’t collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs, and Repa sends nothing off your device. The only network use is the App Store handling your purchase.

Music library

If you import songs from your device’s music library, Repa asks for access to it. The app only reads the songs you pick, and nothing about your library is sent anywhere. You can turn off access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Media & Apple Music.

Songs and files

Songs you import from Files, iCloud Drive, other storage apps or AirDrop are copied into the app on your device. Repa only opens the files you choose. Your songs are never uploaded or shared.

Marks, playlists and settings

Your marks, loops, playlists and per-song settings are stored locally on your device. Imported songs and this data are included in your device’s iCloud or computer backups, handled by Apple, so they come back if you restore your device. Everything is removed when you delete the app.

Purchase

Repa is free to download and keeps 1 song. One purchase, Repa Unlimited, removes the limit. It is bought through the App Store, which is handled entirely by Apple, and Repa itself sends nothing. We don’t receive your name, payment details or any other personal information. See Apple’s privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They contain no personal information. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Data retention and deletion

Since we don’t collect any data, there is nothing for us to keep or delete.
