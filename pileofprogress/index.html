Free to try with up to 10 model types, then pay once. No subscription, no account, no ads, no tracking.

How it works

  1. Set up your stages

    Start with Not finished and Finished, or build your own workflow of up to 12 stages, such as Built, Primed, Painted and Based.

  2. Add your pile

    Make a collection for each game, with armies or teams inside it, and add each kind of mini you own with how many are at each stage.

  3. Move them along

    As you work, move minis to the next stage. Every level shows how many you have and how many are finished.

Made for the pile of shame

  • Collections and armies

    Group your minis by game or range, and inside it by army or team, such as Necrons in 40k.

  • Your own workflow

    Rename, reorder, add and remove stages. One workflow covers all your collections.

  • Progress at a glance

    The whole pile, each collection, each army and each kind of mini show their count and percent finished on a bar from red to green.

  • Notes and photos

    Keep paint recipes and other notes on any level, and a photo of each kind of mini from the camera or your library.

  • Rearrange freely

    Move a kind of mini to another collection or army, and its counts, photo and notes go with it.

  • Offline and private

    No sign-up, and no internet needed except to buy or restore the unlock. Your pile lives on your phone and can be part of your phone’s backup. The app follows light and dark mode.

Free to try, bought once

Everything works in the free app, with up to 10 model types in your whole pile and as many minis of each as you own. One purchase, Unlock the whole pile, lets you add as many model types as you like. There’s no subscription. You can restore it on a new phone from the same store account, and on iPhone it can be shared with your family. A purchase on iPhone doesn’t carry over to Android, or the other way round.

If you used the app before it had this limit, updating it leaves your pile unlimited.

Your pile stays yours

Pile of Progress has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of ours, and we run no server for it: the app sends us nothing. What you add stays on your device, unless your own device backup copies it. The App Store or Google Play handles the purchase. The privacy policy has the details, including what Google’s billing code on Android sends to Google.
