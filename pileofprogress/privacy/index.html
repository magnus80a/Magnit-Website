Pile of Progress Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Pile of Progress is made by MagnIT AB, a Swedish company. Everything you add to Pile of Progress stays on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, so your collections, notes and photos never reach us. The app has no ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs, and we don’t sell data.

What Pile of Progress stores on your device

  • Your pile: the names of your collections, armies and units, and how many miniatures of each unit are at each stage.
  • Your workflow: the names and order of your stages.
  • Notes: the text you write on a collection, army or unit, such as a paint scheme.
  • Photos: the one photo you can give each unit.

All of this is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. We can’t see it, and Pile of Progress doesn’t send it anywhere. It works without an internet connection.

Camera and photos

Pile of Progress asks for camera access the first time you take a photo of a unit. Without it, the app still works, but you can’t take photos in it. You can withdraw it at any time in your device’s settings. Pile of Progress doesn’t use the microphone.

When you pick a photo from your library instead, your device’s own photo picker hands over only the photo you choose, not the rest of your library. Pile of Progress saves a square, smaller copy of it in its own storage and leaves the original in your library as it is.

Device backups

Pile of Progress’s data, including your photos, can be part of your device’s own backup: iCloud on iPhone, and Google’s backup on Android. If you’ve turned these backups on, copies are stored in your Apple or Google account under Apple’s or Google’s privacy policy. We have no access to them. You can turn these backups off in your device’s settings. Android backs up at most 25 MB per app; above that, none of Pile of Progress’s data is backed up.

App stores

Apple and Google handle your download of Pile of Progress under their own privacy policies. They give us statistics about installs, and may give us crash reports from people who’ve agreed to share them. These don’t include your collections, notes or photos.

Who we share data with

Nobody. We don’t receive your data, so we have nothing to share or sell. Apple and Google handle data as described above, in their own services.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. In the app, you can remove a unit’s photo, or delete a unit, an army or a collection with everything in it, photos included. Deleting the app removes all of Pile of Progress’s data from your device. We can’t delete it for you or get it back, because we don’t have it.

Copies in your iCloud or Google backup are kept and deleted according to those services, and you can delete them there.

Your rights

You control your data in Pile of Progress directly, in the app. Because we hold no records about you, there’s nothing for us to copy, correct or delete, but you’re welcome to email us with questions. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY) at imy.se.

Security

Pile of Progress’s data is kept in app storage that other apps can’t read, and your device’s lock protects it. The app doesn’t add a lock or encryption of its own, so anyone who can unlock your device can open it.

Children

Pile of Progress is a general hobby app. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
